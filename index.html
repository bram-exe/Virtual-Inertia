Summary & Abstract

Project abstract as written for the project charter.

We are developing a market mechanism for buying and selling virtual inertia on the power grid. The purpose is to visualize imbursement for inertia supplying resources for their contributions to grid stability. The design will include an in depth report, as well as a physical representation of the model design. The report will include pricing, communication, and other aspects that dictate an ancillary market. We are working with Grid United to develop the mechanim, with our home base in the CSU Powerhouse campus.
