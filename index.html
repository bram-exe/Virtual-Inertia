Project Summary

We are developing a market mechanism for buying and selling virtual inertia. The design will include an in depth report, as well as a physical representation of the model design. The report will include pricing, communication, and other aspects that dictate an ancillary market. We are working with Grid United to develop the mechanim, with our home base in the CSU Powerhouse campus.
